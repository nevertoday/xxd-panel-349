## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one small island, centred and a size smaller than instinct suggests, floating in a large clean white field with more headroom above than below and nothing touching any edge or the fold: the subject plus only the patch of ground under it, rebuilt entirely as a flat silhouette of a few large rounded-rectangle blocks, each one flat colour, stacked horizontally and slightly offset so they overlap, with no outline, no volume, no light or reflection and no photographic texture left anywhere on the subject. The upper part is bright blocks taken from the photograph's most vivid colour, the bottom is a heavy dark base taken from its deepest colour, with a few mid-tone blocks between; the island's edges break outward into a sparse ring of small squares that get smaller and fewer further out, using only those colours. Thin white reserved lines run straight down through the dark blocks and occasionally turn at right angles, like water running off the surface; very thin parts (rails, wires, legs) are a single dark stroke. Each block is laid evenly like marker watercolour, with a slightly darker tide line at its edge and a darker tone where two blocks overlap. When the photograph is a scene, keep only its most representative single object or fragment. The one short handwritten line sits small at the lower right of the island, clearly apart from the scattered squares, in the base's dark colour, in spaced small hand-lettered capitals or thin handwriting. Reject a full-scene copy, a filled background, a subject that keeps its photographic look with only the ground turned into blocks, a tidy pixel grid, outlined forms, squares scattered across the whole canvas, realistic watercolour washes, a drawn horizon or sky, too many colours, and white lines that read as random highlights.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-349/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
